Where does the river of memory start?
Deep in the chambers of the beating heart.
It flows through valleys of forgotten years,
And fills the oceans of our hidden tears.
The sun awakes in robes of burning gold,
To tell the stories that were never told.
Upon the mountain where the eagles fly,
The ancient secrets whisper to the sky.
A silver moon looks down upon the deep,
And sings the weary universe to sleep.
The constellations dance their cosmic dance,
While mortals watch them in a waking trance.
From grains of sand upon the lonely shore,
To galaxies that spiral evermore,
The universe expands in quiet grace,
A masterpiece of time and endless space.
We walk the path of shadows and of light,
Searching for meaning in the endless night.
The winds of change blow fiercely through the trees,
Carrying whispers on the autumn breeze.

The constellations dance their cosmic dance,
While mortals watch them in a waking trance.
From grains of sand upon the lonely shore,
To galaxies that spiral evermore,
The universe expands in quiet grace,
A masterpiece of time and endless space.
We walk the path of shadows and of light,
Searching for meaning in the endless night.
The winds of change blow fiercely through the trees,
Carrying whispers on the autumn breeze.

To tell the stories that were never told.
